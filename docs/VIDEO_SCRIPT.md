# Demo video — narration

> **Status.** This is the script of the **earlier synthetic-voice reference cut** (runtime
> 4:01.56). It is kept as the record of how the film was written and marked up. It is *not*
> the delivered narration: the submitted film is a human read, runs **4:49.70**, and its
> wording and timings both moved. For the delivered text and times, read the caption track
> embedded in the film itself; for what the film contains and is allowed to claim, read
> [VIDEO_MEDIA_MANIFEST.md](VIDEO_MEDIA_MANIFEST.md).

The spoken track, in order, with the second each line starts and the picture it plays over.

The reference audio was macOS `say` (Samantha, 166 wpm) — a stand-in, not a performance.
A silent cut and the timings below existed so the film could be re-voiced without touching
the picture: read to these marks and the edit still lands. That is exactly what then
happened, and the human read was timed against its own clock.

**Tone.** A student explaining something they built, not a launch film. Contractions,
understatement, and three jokes that are allowed to be quiet. Every number spoken is a
number the real app put on screen in the same shot — nothing is rounded up, and nothing
is described that the picture does not show.

## Lines that were cut, and why

- **"It would rather lose the order than give me something I said I did not want."** The
  recording says otherwise: Pool takes the member's units out and the order *re-forms
  without them*, to a whole case, and the declaration goes back to standing. The line
  became "it would rather leave me out", which is what the screen actually shows.
- **A second Pool logo at 0:18.** The mark appeared at the end of the inversion and again
  at the reveal twenty-six seconds later. The first one went; "So I built Pool" is the
  moment the brand is allowed to arrive.
- **Run ids, planner iterations, tool names, CloudFront.** All real, all in the repo, none
  of them something a judge should be asked to remember from a four-minute film.

## Regenerating

`script.json` in the video working directory holds the cue text and the silence before
each line; `timeline.py` turns it into the beat table, the SRT and the caption cards, and
`build.py` cuts the picture to it. Change a line and everything downstream moves with it.
